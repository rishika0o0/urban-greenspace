End-to-End Deep Learning Architecture

The 6-Stage Satellite Data Pipeline

From raw multispectral top-of-atmosphere surface reflectance to an interactive production-grade geospatial intelligence dashboard.

01

GEE Acquisition

Connects to Google Earth Engine API, loads Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface reflectance, filters <10% cloud cover, applies SCL mask, and outputs an 11-band median composite.

earthengine-api Cloud Mask Median Composite
02

Tile Generation

Slices the GeoTIFF composite into 256×256 px chips. Discards tiles with <70% valid data, generating 11-channel float32 tensors and RGB preview PNGs.

Rasterio 256×256 Chips Float32 Tensors
03

NDVI Auto-Labelling

Calculates (B8 - B4) / (B8 + B4). Tiles with mean NDVI > 0.30 are categorized as green canopy (forests, parks, tree-lined boulevards) vs non-green urban.

NDVI > 0.30 Auto Annotation Preview Borders
04

ResNet-18 Training

Modified ResNet-18 expanded from 3 to 11 input channels. Uses weighted cross-entropy for 6:1 class imbalance, AdamW optimiser, and Cosine Annealing learning rate.

PyTorch 11-Band Conv1 Weighted Loss
05

Model Evaluation

Exhaustive statistical validation producing per-class precision, recall, confusion matrix, confidence distributions, and training loss convergence curves.

97.63% Acc Confusion Matrix F1 Analytics
06

Streamlit Dashboard

Interactive production platform featuring Folium map layer controllers, styled HTML popups, KPI summary cards, full tile gallery, and CSV report export.

Urban Forestry & Ecology

Preserving Bengaluru's Garden City Heritage

Rapid urbanization has reduced Bangalore's historical tree canopy from over 68% in the 1970s to under 15% today. This deep learning platform arms municipal planners, research institutes, and conservationists with continuous, satellite-verified canopy monitoring.

🌳

Urban Heat Island (UHI) Mitigation

Pinpoints micro-climates suffering from thermal stress due to loss of tree canopy shade.

🗺️

Ward-Level Green Auditing

Objective 10m-resolution spatial evidence for BBMP and forest department re-greening initiatives.

🔬

Sentinel-2 Temporal Tracking

5-day revisit rate enables dynamic quarterly tracking without expensive airborne LIDAR surveys.
